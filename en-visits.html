What we check

Five groups covering technical points, visible conditions and the small signs that can appear when a home has been empty for a while.

Water

  • Main shut-off valve and meter
  • Leaks under sinks, basins and washing machine
  • Traps and drains
  • Water heater or boiler: pressure and dripping
  • Stains and damp on ceilings and walls

Electricity and refrigeration

  • Electrical panel and RCD test
  • Fridge and freezer
  • Supply status
  • Meter readings when relevant

Closure and security

  • Doors, locks and signs of forced entry
  • Windows, shutters and awnings
  • Alarm status, if installed
  • Access points to the property

Environment

  • Cross ventilation
  • Relative humidity with hygrometer
  • Mould in cupboards and hidden areas
  • Signs of pests

Other

  • Watering plants
  • Terrace or balcony check
  • Furniture check to spot or prevent damage
  • Mail and advertising collection
  • Removal of notices that reveal the home is empty
  • Follow-up on items from the previous visit

After every visit, you receive a report.

You do not just get a “everything is fine” message. We record what was checked, what changed and what needs follow-up.

  • Date, time and duration
  • Checklist by area
  • Dated photos and observations
  • Incidents and actions taken
  • Items to follow up on the next visit
A report with dated photos can serve as supporting documentation after an incident or for an insurance claim. Each insurer decides which documentation it accepts and which coverage applies.

A history that helps document what happened.

If damp suddenly appears, a shutter breaks or there is a leak, you can check how the property looked during previous visits. This history does not replace an expert assessment, but it can help document the condition and how it changed.
