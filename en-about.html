About us

HouseWatchers.es comes from having been on both sides.

From managing homes, and from knowing what it feels like to leave your own home far away for months.

Experience managing homes

I’m Cristina Palacios. I was co-founder and COO of Aptuno, a residential rental platform operating in Bogotá and Mexico City.

During that time I ran operations end to end: owner relationships, visit coordination, contract closing and signing, customer support and property management. That experience is the operational foundation of HouseWatchers.es.

And I also know what it is like to be far away

I know how unsettling it can feel to leave your home closed for months without anyone nearby who can get in, check it or react if something happens.

HouseWatchers.es exists to solve exactly that: giving you a trusted person in Barcelona who can look after access to your home and help when you are not here.

How we work

Few promises, plenty of traceability: securely held keys, checklist-based visits when you book them, dated photos, follow-up and clearly quoted one-off services. HouseWatchers.es operates in Barcelona city.
