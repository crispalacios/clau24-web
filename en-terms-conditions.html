2. Scope of services

HouseWatchers.es provides key holding, authorised property access, preventive/additional visits, technician/provider assistance, property preparation and third-party coordination.

HouseWatchers.es currently operates exclusively within Barcelona city. The property must be located within the municipality of Barcelona.

3. Registration and contracting

A request made through the website, WhatsApp, phone or email does not by itself create a final contract. HouseWatchers.es confirms the service, scope, total price and, where applicable, date/time window.

The customer confirms that they own the property or are sufficiently authorised to contract and provide the keys.

4. Plans and fees

Keys, Essential and Peace of mind plans include the items shown on the website or in the service confirmation.

Where annual discounted billing is offered, duration, renewal and price are provided before contracting.

5. Key holding and authorised persons

Customers must keep the authorised-person list up to date.

HouseWatchers.es may perform reasonable identity checks and pause a handover or access where authorisation is unclear.

6. Property access and provider assistance

Property access means attending with the held keys, opening the property and allowing access. It does not include waiting inside.

Technician/provider assistance includes attending, opening, waiting for the included period, checking completion and closing. Extra waiting time is charged at the applicable rate.

7. Scheduled, urgent and out-of-hours services

Scheduled: requested at least 24 hours in advance.

Urgent/same day: subject to availability. No specific arrival time is guaranteed unless expressly confirmed.

Standard hours: 08:00–20:00. 20:00–23:00: +€25. 23:00–08:00: +€50. Sundays/public holidays: +€25. Where surcharges overlap, only the highest is charged.

8. Not a locksmith or private security service

HouseWatchers.es enters using the spare keys previously left in custody. We do not force doors or manipulate locks.

If a lock is broken or a key does not work, a locksmith will be required and charged separately.

HouseWatchers.es does not provide surveillance, alarm-receiving-centre services or guaranteed alarm response.

9. Third-party providers

Where HouseWatchers.es coordinates a technician, cleaner or other provider, the customer will approve the relevant scope or quote where required.

Unless stated otherwise, the technical/material work is carried out by the third party. HouseWatchers.es remains responsible for its own coordination in accordance with law.

10. Guests and HomeExchange

Customers may authorise family, guests or HomeExchange participants to receive keys or access the property.

Additional key handovers/collections are charged separately and out-of-hours services carry the applicable surcharge.

HouseWatchers.es is not responsible for how authorised guests use the property, without prejudice to liability for HouseWatchers.es’s own acts.

Before the customer is bound, the total price including applicable taxes and surcharges will be confirmed.

Purchases, repairs, locksmiths, materials and third-party services are not included unless expressly stated.
