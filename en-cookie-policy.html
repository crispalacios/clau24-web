Cookie policy

Information about cookies and similar technologies on clau24.com.

1. Current situation

Clau24 currently does not intentionally deploy analytics, behavioural advertising or marketing cookies.

Hosting, security or technical providers may use strictly necessary mechanisms. Necessary technical cookies do not require consent.

2. What cookies are

Cookies are small files or mechanisms that can be stored or accessed on a user’s device to enable technical functions, remember preferences or, in other cases, analyse behaviour.

3. Non-essential cookies

If analytics, advertising or other consent-requiring cookies are introduced later, this policy will be updated and a consent mechanism will allow users to accept or reject them before installation.

4. Third-party services

The website may link to external services such as WhatsApp, which are subject to their own policies.

5. Changes

This policy will be reviewed when the technologies used on the site change.
