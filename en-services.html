How pricing works

Scheduled: requested at least 24 hours in advance.

Urgent / same day: a service requested to be carried out on the same day.

Standard hours: 08:00–20:00. 20:00–23:00: +€25. 23:00–08:00: +€50. Sundays and public holidays: +€25. If more than one surcharge applies, only the highest one is charged. Urgent services are subject to availability.

Clau24 is not a locksmith service. We attend with the spare keys previously left with us by the owner. We do not force doors or manipulate locks. If the lock is broken or the key does not work, a locksmith will be required and that cost is charged separately.

Repairs and renovations: we can coordinate those too.

We can find providers, compare quotes and coordinate the work. Our management fee is quoted according to scope; the repair or renovation itself is paid separately according to the provider quote you approve. If you want us to attend the property during the work, supervision visits are charged separately at €70 per visit.
